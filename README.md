One funny line, short enough to read in a breath and whole enough to land on its own: an AI asked to act more human took the request a little too literally.

> I asked my AI to act more human. Now it ignores my texts and blames the Wi-Fi.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3 standard library only) writes the line to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`, checked first against the competition gate: 1 to 140 characters of UTF-8 text, a single line.

```sh
python3 main.py
```
